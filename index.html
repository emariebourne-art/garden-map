Picking etiquette

  • Only pick what hangs over the sidewalk or street. Never enter a yard.
  • Check the notes. If the owner asked people to leave it, leave it.
  • Take a share, not the lot. Leave some for the next person and the birds.
  • Don’t post names, house numbers, or anything about the people who live there.
